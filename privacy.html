Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyMacrosFast ("MyMacrosFast," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the MyMacrosFast mobile application (the "App"). MyMacrosFast is operated by Hadi Ramlaoui, an individual based in California, USA.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Meal photos and descriptions. When you log a meal, you may take or upload a photo of your food, or type a text description of it. We use this to estimate the food items, portion sizes, and macronutrient/calorie content of your meal.
  • Account information. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive basic identity information from that provider (such as your name and email address, as permitted by your privacy settings with that provider) to create and secure your account. When you sign in with Apple, we also keep the sign-in token Apple issues to us, solely so that we can revoke your Sign in with Apple authorization with Apple if you later delete your account (Apple requires this). You can also use the App without signing in — in that case, your data is tied to an anonymous, device-local session rather than a personal identity.
  • Body and goal details. If you use the Daily Goal feature, you can enter your sex, age, height, weight, activity level, and goal (for example, lose or maintain weight). We use these only to calculate your suggested daily calorie and macronutrient targets. They are stored with your account so your targets follow you across devices. You can change them at any time from the Account screen, and they are removed with everything else when you delete your account or data.

Information generated by the App

  • Logged entries. The meals you log, including estimated macros (protein, fat, carbs, calories), food names, portion weights, and timestamps.
  • Barcode scans and food searches. When you scan a product barcode or search the food database, the barcode number or your search term is used to look up that product and its nutrition information from external food-data providers (see "How We Share Information" below).
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to MyMacrosFast Premium, we record whether your subscription is active so the App can unlock Premium features. We receive this from Apple through RevenueCat (see below); we never see your payment card details.
  • Technical/diagnostic data. We use Sentry, an error and crash-reporting service, to help us identify and fix bugs. It collects technical details about the crash or error (such as device type, OS version, and the part of the App that failed) but is not used to collect your meal data, and we do not attach your account identity to crash reports.

We do not currently use behavioral analytics or advertising tracking of any kind.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Analyze meal photos/descriptions and estimate their macronutrient and calorie content
  • Look up product and nutrition information for barcodes you scan and foods you search for
  • Store your logged meals so you can review your history
  • Sync your data across devices when you're signed in
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App
  • Diagnose and fix technical problems

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as necessary to provide the App:

  • Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI models): meal photos and text descriptions you submit, and the messages you send to the Maccy assistant (together with the logged meal or day Maccy is helping you with), are sent to Anthropic's API solely to identify food items, estimate macros, and answer your request. Anthropic processes this data as our service provider under their commercial API terms and does not use it to train its models.
  • Supabase: our backend infrastructure provider. Supabase hosts our database, file storage, and authentication systems, and stores your account data, logged entries, and meal photos on our behalf.
  • Food-data providers (Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central): to identify a scanned or searched food and its nutrition, we query public food databases. When you scan a barcode, your device contacts Open Food Facts directly, so that provider may receive the barcode number and standard network information (such as your IP address and a general app identifier). Food searches — and barcodes not found in Open Food Facts — are sent through our backend to USDA FoodData Central, which receives the search term or barcode but not your device's IP address. We do not send your account information, meal history, or any personal identifiers to these providers.
  • Google / Apple: if you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers share basic identity information with us as part of the sign-in process, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: if you purchase a subscription, that transaction is handled by Apple's In-App Purchase system. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status: it receives your App Store transaction details and a random app user identifier (your account's internal ID, not your name or email) so that your subscription can be matched to your account and restored on a new device. We do not see or store your payment card details — Apple handles billing directly.
  • Sentry: our crash-reporting provider (see "Technical/diagnostic data" above). Crash reports contain technical details about the error, not your meal data or account identity.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MyMacrosFast, our users, or others.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your logged meals and account data are retained for as long as you keep using the App.

Moving anonymous data to an account. If you start using the App without signing in and later sign in with Google or Apple, the App asks whether to bring the meals from your anonymous session over to your account. If you choose to, that data (meals, photos, pinned meals, and — only if your account has none yet — your goal and body details) is moved to your account; nothing is copied elsewhere. If you choose not to, it stays under the anonymous session and is not linked to your account.

You can permanently delete your data at any time from the Account screen in the App:

  • If you're signed in, "Delete My Account and Data" permanently deletes your account, every meal you've logged, any photos you've uploaded, your goal and body details, and — if you signed in with Apple — revokes your Sign in with Apple authorization and removes the token described above.
  • If you're using the App anonymously, "Delete My Data" does the same for your anonymous session's data.

This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Data is removed from our live systems immediately; residual copies in routine backups are purged automatically within a limited period afterwards. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — Apple manages billing, so cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings (the App links you there before deletion).

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Regardless of where you live, you can contact us at any time to:

  • Request deletion of your information (or use the in-app deletion feature described above)
